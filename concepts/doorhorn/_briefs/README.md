# Writer briefs

Each file here is the brief that produced (or should produce) one document of the Doorhorn package. If a document is missing or needs a rewrite, hand the brief to a subagent or write it yourself. Every brief assumes these inputs exist in the repository:

- `concepts/tournament/WINNER.md` — the winning concept synthesis (source of truth)
- `concepts/tournament/digest/` — research digests and top-concept dossiers (regenerate with `node concepts/tournament/extract-digests.mjs`)

Common rules for every document: working title "Doorhorn (working title)" on first mention; keep cast names and phases exactly as in WINNER.md; no licensed IP (no names, characters, logos, quotes or trade dress from Bob's Burgers, South Park, American Dad, Marvel or any real franchise; no real agencies, insignia or call signs); tone target is warm ensemble comedy at a 4+/9+ rating; facts keep their source URLs from the digests or from a fresh, cited web search; plain, specific, professional prose with no jokes in the document's own voice; markdown with headings and tables.
