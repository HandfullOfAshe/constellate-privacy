# Aesthetic research: borrowing the energy of Bob's Burgers / South Park / American Dad / MCU Infinity War-Endgame marketing for an ORIGINAL iOS app style, plus the 2026 iOS (26/27 Liquid Glass) design context, legally embeddable display fonts, and vibrant indie iOS apps praised for design in 2025-2026

The four reference properties share a small set of transferable, non-infringing design principles. South Park is built from simple geometric shapes in primary/secondary colours, deliberately jerky motion, and near-identical child silhouettes distinguished only by hat, hair and clothing colour; its look is literally "digitally reconstructed construction paper" (scanned pilot textures reused in Maya), and its one-week production cycle is what makes topical satire possible. Bob's Burgers pairs Jay Howell's lanky, loose, zine-punk character line (influenced by Pink Panther/Hanna-Barbera cel colours, later "Simpsonized") with Sirron Norris's pastel-heavy San Francisco Victorian storefront backgrounds; its humour is warm and character-driven rather than shock-based, music is structural (fantasy numbers, diegetic songs, credits), and it has a memorable black-outline-on-white credits motif. American Dad's creators describe it as "very bright, very easy on the eyes," with comedy from character and story ("the human condition") plus farce and surrealism; critics fault MacFarlane-style limited animation (only mouths move), which is a warning, not a model. MCU marketing contributes ensemble/stakes grammar: 32 "Avenge the Fallen" posters where colour = alive and black-and-white = dusted, team posters colour-coded to the six Infinity Stones, a franchise logo whose core "A-arrow" stays fixed while texture and colour shift per film (purple for Thanos, red for Ultron), condensed all-caps custom lettering (closest match: Avengeance, commercial licence required) contrasted with a classical serif subtitle, and taglines like "Whatever it takes."

On platform: Liquid Glass (June 2025) is now non-optional. iOS 27 (announced 8 June 2026, shipped 14 September 2026) removes the iOS 26 compatibility opt-out, adds a user slider from ultra-clear to fully tinted, sharper layered icons, and SwiftUI reorderable containers, swipe actions on any view, prominent tab roles and toolbar minimisation. Apple's own guidance is unusually explicit for a colourful app: keep glass in the navigation layer, never stack glass on glass, tint only the primary action, and "if you want to imbue colour into your app, do it in the content layer." Custom fonts must implement Dynamic Type and Bold Text; iOS minimum text is 11 pt (default 17 pt). Live Activities should use medium-or-heavier text and suit activities under eight hours.

Fonts are the biggest legal trap. Envato Elements fonts may not be "incorporated or distributed within an End Product," and Adobe Fonts "does not allow you to embed the fonts within mobile or desktop applications"; both are fine for rasterised logos/marketing art only. Google Fonts under the OFL (Bangers, Fredoka, Lilita One, Titan One, Baloo 2, Anton) or Apache (Luckiest Guy) can be bundled in a paid app with the licence file included. Apple's SF fonts may not be embedded and SF Symbols are for in-app UI only.

Market signals favour the direction: 2026 Apple Design Awards rewarded "hand-drawn simplicity" (grug), a "Saturday morning cartoon" puzzler (Is This Seat Taken?) and Not Boring Camera's "big bold design"; 2025 honoured a "cartoon musical comedy" game and a paper-cutout karaoke musical. Not Boring Software shows design alone can carry a paid business, using game-engine UI and a dozen sound variants per button. Suno commercial rights require Pro/Premier and, from 3 September 2026, only officially downloaded tracks within monthly caps (Pro 20, Premier 60) count.

## Key facts (sourced)
- South Park's look descends from Terry Gilliam's Monty Python cutouts; characters are 'simple geometrical shapes and primary and secondary colors', child characters share the same size and shape and differ only by 'clothing, hair and skin colors, and headwear', movement is 'intentionally jerky', and episodes are finished in about one week (as little as 3-4 days) which enables topical satire.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Park
- South Park Studios states the show is animated mostly in Maya, that the pilot's construction paper 'was then scanned into a massive computer database, which animators now use', and that the result is 'digitally reconstructed construction paper' using 'those SAME TEXTURES' for 16+ years.
  Source: https://southpark.cc.com/news/712d55/faq-do-you-still-use-construction-paper-to-animate-the-show
- Bob's Burgers character designer Jay Howell says his style is recognisable by 'lanky features and twisted yet oddly optimistic expressions'; he cites Pink Panther ('the colors and the animation still really get me'), Hanna-Barbera cel colours, Garfield, Far Side and MAD as influences; his original Belchers 'had bigger noses and were skinnier' and were 'Simpsonized to the max' for broadcast.
  Source: https://atodmagazine.com/2022/05/31/jay-howell-cartoonist/
- Howell developed his 'loose, weird style' in Bay Area punk/zine culture; Louise's pink bunny ears became the show's iconic merchandise marker ('I broke my brain trying to make the most iconic family').
  Source: https://hifructose.com/2025/11/25/punks-git-cut-the-art-of-jay-howell/
- Bob's Burgers backgrounds were created by muralist Sirron Norris, 'highlighting his signature style of Victorian homes and storefronts of San Francisco'; the show's look is 'heavily influenced by the Mission District'; his mural technique uses 'a bold, pastel-heavy palette and jaunty compositions'.
  Source: http://www.sirronnorris.com/bobs-burgers
- Bob's Burgers is character-driven with 'less emphasis on shock comedy' (seen as a spiritual successor to King of the Hill); musical moments appear 'in fantasy sequences, as diegetic music, or over the episode's closing credits'; the closing credits are drawn 'with a black outline over a white background and the characters in full color'; the 'Burger of the Day' pun is a signature hallmark.
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob%27s_Burgers
- Loren Bouchard: Bob's Burgers combines 'a family and a workplace all in one'; episodes take 'no less than nine months and sometimes a year'; characters never age and live 'the same year over and over again'; Louise's hat is a security blanket signalling vulnerability under toughness.
  Source: https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/bobs-burgers-loren-bouchard-jeff-kleinsmith-sub-pop-animation-110517
- American Dad co-creator Matt Weitzman describes the show's visuals as 'all very bright, very easy on the eyes'; ~50 in-house Fox animators handle poses, models, props and storyboards while colouring is done in Korea.
  Source: https://www.awn.com/animationworld/american-dad-touchdown
- American Dad's comedy derives 'from the human condition and emotions that everyone can relate to' rather than cutaway gags, using farce, surrealism, plot twists and black comedy; the ensemble mixes a CIA dad, homemaker, liberal daughter, nerd son, a flamboyant alien (Roger) and a goldfish with a human brain (Klaus).
  Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Dad!
- Cartoon Brew's critique of MacFarlane animation: it 'consists almost entirely of character's mouths moving, with the occasional rigid pose-to-pose animation' with 'the same exact timing on every movement' — a cautionary example of what NOT to replicate in motion design.
  Source: https://www.cartoonbrew.com/tv/analyzing-the-animation-of-seth-macfarlane-7429.html
- Endgame's 32 'Avenge the Fallen' character posters used 'a simple color code: those active ... are in full color, while those turned to dust are black and white'; the tagline redefines the team name and calls back to 'we'll avenge it'.

## Implications for the concept
- Original visual recipe that captures the tone without copying: South Park's economy (uniform body silhouettes differentiated only by hat/hair/colour, flat fills, hard geometric shapes, a scanned paper-grain texture) makes a large ORIGINAL ensemble cheap for a solo dev; layer Bob's Burgers warmth (thin loose line, pastel-heavy storefront backgrounds, black-outline-on-white 'credits' motif) on top, and reserve American Dad-style saturated primaries for hero moments. Avoid limited 'only the mouth moves' animation; use short squash-and-stretch or cutout-jitter loops instead.
- Steal the MCU's grammar, not its assets: a fixed wordmark whose colour shifts per season/chapter; roster art colour-coded by faction; 'in colour = active, greyscale = fallen' as a UI state language (e.g. streaks alive vs broken, teammates who missed a day); an all-caps condensed display face paired with a contrasting serif for subtitles; short imperative taglines ('Whatever it takes'-style) for daily missions. Never use Avengeance/Aviano without paid licences, and keep lettering distinct from the Avengers 'A-arrow' trade dress.
- Liquid Glass is mandatory on iOS 27, so design the cartoon world as the CONTENT layer and let tab bars, toolbars and sheets be system glass. Do not tint every control; tint only the one primary action. Use concentric corner radii, bold left-aligned type, and test the whole UI at both ends of the new user slider (ultra-clear to fully tinted) plus Reduce Motion / Reduce Bright Effects.
- Ship a layered app icon via Icon Composer that still reads in monochrome tinted and clear modes — a flat cartoon mascot silhouette with one strong shape works; detailed multi-colour art will collapse in tinted mode.
- Give music a structural, Bob's-Burgers-style role (diegetic songs, fantasy-number rewards, sung end-of-day credits) and follow Not Boring's audio technique: a dozen micro-variants per tap sound so the app feels physical. Budget Suno on Premier (60 official downloads/month) and keep download receipts, since only officially downloaded tracks are commercially cleared; avoid artist-name prompts because there is no indemnification.
- Fonts: bundle only OFL/Apache Google Fonts (Bangers for comic punch, Luckiest Guy or Titan One for cartoon headlines, Fredoka or Baloo 2 for friendly UI text with real weight ranges, Anton for condensed blockbuster titles) and include their licence files. Envato Elements and Adobe Fonts may only produce rasterised logos, marketing art and screenshots — their font files cannot ship inside the .ipa. If a specific Envato/Adobe face is essential for in-app text, buy the foundry's app-embedding licence.
- Envato Elements UI kits, illustrations and textures are fine as fully integrated, non-extractable parts of one app (one licence per app), but the 'on-demand' prohibition means you cannot build an avatar-maker, card-creator or sticker-customiser out of Envato graphics; original or self-drawn parts are required for any user-customisation feature.
- Custom cartoon fonts must scale with Dynamic Type and respond to Bold Text (iOS 27 finalists and winners are judged on this); keep body text at or above 11 pt and prefer 17 pt, and use a fat rounded face (Fredoka/Baloo 2) rather than a thin decorative one for anything read at length.
- Widgets and Live Activities are the daily-ritual surface: a Live Activity for a timed daily 'mission' (under eight hours) with medium-or-heavier text and a custom background colour that auto-extends full-screen in StandBy; SF Symbols only for in-app iconography, never in the logo.
- Apple's award juries in 2025-2026 explicitly rewarded hand-drawn simplicity, Saturday-morning-cartoon puzzlers, cartoon musical comedy and paper-cutout karaoke — a vibrant, music-driven, comedic cartoon app is on-trend rather than risky; Not Boring's patron tier shows a design-led, personality-first app can sustain premium pricing without feature bloat.

## Open uncertainties
- The exact footer wording of the SF Symbols licence restriction (app icons/logos/trademark use) was not captured verbatim by the fetch; verify on developer.apple.com/sf-symbols/ before relying on the quoted paraphrase.
- Apple's HIG Widgets page did not render in any fetch, so iOS 26/27 widget rendering modes (full-colour vs accented/tinted/clear) and their colour guidance are not confirmed from a primary source in this session.
- Claims that iOS 27 removed the UIDesignRequiresCompatibility opt-out and that Liquid Glass is 'mandatory' come from third-party developer blogs (byteiota, rabinarayanpatra, useyourloaf), not Apple release notes; confirm in the Xcode 27 / iOS 27 release notes.
- Suno's post-3-September-2026 download caps (7/20/60) and the 'official download = commercial gate' reading come from a third-party analysis of Suno's announcement; verify on suno.com/terms-september-2026 and the help centre, and note the unresolved question of whether free-era generations become commercial after a paid download. Copyright may not vest in AI output at all, and UMG/Sony litigation is ongoing.
- Envato's clause allowing a 'web-enabled Font as part of an End Product' is written for websites; whether any reading permits in-app text rendering from bundled font files is untested, so the safe interpretation (no font files in the binary) was adopted.
- Designer credits for Bangers (commonly Vernon Adams), Lilita One (Juan Montoreano) and Titan One (Rodrigo Fuenzalida) were not present in the fetched DESCRIPTION files and are from general knowledge; confirm on each specimen page.
- No primary-source hex palettes exist for the three shows; South Park 'primary and secondary colors', Bob's Burgers 'pastel-heavy', and American Dad 'very bright' are the only sourced characterisations. Infinity Stone hex values circulating online are fan-derived.
- The American Dad palette breakdown found on a colouring-page site (naturalistic colours, ideology-coded family wardrobes) is descriptive fan content, not an authoritative production source.
- Apple's ADA descriptions of grug and Is This Seat Taken? are one-line blurbs; hands-on review of their actual line weight, palette and motion would be needed before using them as direct benchmarks.
- WebSearch quota was exhausted early, so coverage relied on You.com search/content extraction and direct fetches; some JS-rendered Apple HIG pages (Materials, Widgets) and Google Fonts specimen pages returned empty and were substituted with WWDC transcripts, GitHub metadata and Apple newsroom pages.
